PASHUPATINATH TEMPLE

Pashupatinath Temple is Nepal’s most sacred Hindu temple, renowned for its stunning pagoda architecture, rich spiritual heritage.

👁 View Details

LUMBINI

Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, renowned for its sacred monasteries, peaceful gardens, and profound spiritual significance.

JANAKI TEMPLE

Janaki Temple is a beautiful and sacred temple in Janakpur dedicated to Goddess Sita, known for its stunning architecture and religious importance.

CHITWAN NATIONAL PARK

Chitwan National Park is Nepal’s first national park, famous for its rich wildlife, jungle safaris, and endangered one-horned rhinoceroses.

SHREEANTU ILAM

Shree Antu is a scenic hill station in Ilam, famous for its breathtaking sunrise views, lush tea gardens, and peaceful natural beauty.

PATHIVARA TEMPLE

Pathivara Temple is a revered Hindu pilgrimage site in Taplejung, known for its spiritual significance and stunning Himalayan views.

RARA LAKE

Rara Lake is Nepal’s largest lake, renowned for its crystal-clear blue waters, pristine surroundings, and breathtaking mountain scenery.

SWAYAMBHUNATH

Swayambhunath is an ancient Buddhist stupa in Kathmandu, famous for its panoramic city views, sacred atmosphere, and iconic Buddha eyes.

GODAWARI BOTANICAL GARDEN

Godawari Botanical Garden is a peaceful natural sanctuary near Kathmandu, known for its diverse plant species, beautiful landscapes, and relaxing environment.

ANNAPURNA BASE CAMP

Annapurna Base Camp is a world-famous trekking destination offering breathtaking Himalayan views, diverse landscapes, and an unforgettable mountain adventure.
